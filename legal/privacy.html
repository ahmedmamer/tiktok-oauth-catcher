Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Everyday Label ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our website, TikTok integration, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Order information: name, shipping address, email, and order details, collected via our sales platforms (Etsy, TikTok Shop) to fulfill orders.
  • TikTok account information: if you authorize our TikTok app, we may receive basic profile information and permissions you explicitly grant (for example, the ability to publish content on your behalf), limited strictly to what TikTok's authorization flow discloses to you.
  • Analytics: basic usage data from our sales channels' built-in analytics tools.

2. How We Use Information

  • To fulfill and ship orders
  • To operate our TikTok presence, including publishing content when authorized
  • To respond to customer inquiries
  • To improve our products and services

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with service providers necessary to operate our business (e.g., print-on-demand fulfillment partners, payment processors, and TikTok itself for authorized integrations), and only to the extent required to provide the service.

4. Data Retention

We retain order and account data only as long as necessary for business, legal, or tax purposes.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us (see below). You may revoke TikTok app authorization at any time via your TikTok account settings.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, including storing credentials securely and limiting access to authorized use only.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of our Services after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent via our Instagram (@theeverydaylabelstore) or Etsy shop messaging.
